Bridging Compassion & Expertise in Mental Health Recruitment

At ThriveMind Recruiting, we understand that exceptional mental healthcare begins with extraordinary professionals. Founded by clinicians and recruitment experts, we specialize in connecting psychiatrists, PMHNPs, and therapists with progressive healthcare organizations across the US.

Our Story & Mission

We started ThriveMind with a singular vision: to alleviate the behavioral health staffing crisis by bringing high-touch, specialized recruiting to mental healthcare providers nationwide.

Our Mission

To elevate the standard of behavioral health staffing through rigorous vetting, deep clinical insight, and meaningful cultural alignment between clinicians and care facilities.

Our Vision

To be the premier national recruitment partner for psychiatric hospitals, outpatient clinics, and wellness centers seeking top-tier mental health professionals.
